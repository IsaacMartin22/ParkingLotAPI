Parking Lot API Documentation

Base URL: https://api-service-i1ms.onrender.com

This service exposes parking lot inventory, floor and section lookup, parking-space mutation, analytics, diagnostics, chat, contact, and integration endpoints.

Public writes are limited to parking-space updates, analytics events, contact messages, and chat. Lot, floor, and section creation is handled outside the public API.

Capacity limits are enforced in the domain: lots may have up to 6 floors, floors up to 10 sections, and sections up to 10 parking spaces.

GET POST PUT DELETE

Parking Lots

Top-level parking lot lookup and nested floor details.

MethodEndpointDescription
GET/api/lotsGet all parking lots.
GET/api/lots/{id}Get a parking lot by ID.
GET/api/lots/{lotId}/floors/{floorId}/detailsGet floor details for a specific lot and floor.

Floors

Floor-level data within a parking lot.

MethodEndpointDescription
GET/api/floorsGet all floors.
GET/api/floors/{id}Get a floor by ID.

Sections

Sections group parking spaces on a floor.

MethodEndpointDescription
GET/api/sectionsGet all sections.
GET/api/sections/{id}Get a section by ID.

Parking Spaces

Parking-space lookup and occupancy updates.

MethodEndpointDescription
GET/api/spacesGet all parking spaces.
GET/api/spaces/{id}Get a parking space by ID.
PUT/api/spaces/{id}Assign a car to an empty parking space.
DELETE/api/spaces/{id}Remove a car from an occupied parking space.

Analytics

Record events and query aggregated analytics.

MethodEndpointDescription
POST/api/analyticsRecord an analytics event.
POST/api/analytics/searchQuery analytics with filters.

Chat and Contact

User-facing support endpoints.

MethodEndpointDescription
GET/api/recent-interactionsGet recent chatbot interactions.
POST/api/chatSubmit a chat question.
POST/api/contactSend a contact message.

Diagnostics, Integrations, and Events

Operational visibility, deployment info, and SSE updates.

MethodEndpointDescription
